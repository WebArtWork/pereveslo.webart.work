# База відпочинку «Перевесло»

Live site: https://pereveslo.webart.work

## About
«Перевесло» is a countryside retreat (база відпочинку) located in the village of Orynyn, at vul. Cheberсeva 2, near Kamianets-Podilskyi. The property positions itself as a place to combine several formats of rest in one visit, from a day trip to an overnight stay, built around lodging, Ukrainian cuisine, water activities, wellness, and event hosting. The page is explicit that some details (room counts, pricing, exact distances) are not yet confirmed and should be checked directly with the property.

## Amenities
- Lodging (проживання) on site
- Ukrainian cuisine and catering for events
- Sauna and a wood-fired bathhouse (баня), plus a separate hot tub (чан)
- Boats and catamarans
- Fishing on the property
- Gazebos (альтанки) for outdoor gatherings
- Banquet hall for events, with a stated capacity of up to 250 guests
- Live music available as a service for events
